# IMO 2026 Problems (English)

## Summary

The official English problem set of the 67th International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO 2026), held in Shanghai, China, on 15–16 July 2026. Six problems, worth 7 points each, over two days of 4 hours 30 minutes each. The set spans number theory (Problems 1 and 6), Euclidean geometry (Problem 2), combinatorial game theory (Problems 3 and 4), and functional equations (Problem 5).

The PDF itself contains only the six statements and the exam times; all other facts below are compiled from web research and are marked accordingly.

## Quotes & Evidence

- Problem 1 move: "replaces these two integers with $\gcd(m, n)$ and $\frac{\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)}{\gcd(m,n)}$".
- Problem 5 chain, for every $x, y \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$: $\sqrt{\frac{x^2 + f(y)^2}{2}} \ge \frac{f(x) + y}{2} \ge \sqrt{x\,f(y)}$.
- Problem 6 conclusion: there exist positive integers $T, L$ such that $a_{n+T} = a_n + L$ for every positive integer $n$.
